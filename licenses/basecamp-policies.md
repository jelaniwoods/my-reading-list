# Policy text provenance

The Terms and Privacy text in `config/locales/en.yml`, rendered by `app/views/pages/terms.html.erb` and
`privacy.html.erb`, adapts the [Basecamp open-source policies by 37signals LLC](https://github.com/basecamp/policies).
The source revision is `d39f6f17bbd90d82b1df9fa7a0df845edd487de0` (2023-12-26):

- [Terms](https://github.com/basecamp/policies/blob/d39f6f17bbd90d82b1df9fa7a0df845edd487de0/terms/index.md),
  dated 2023-01-06.
- [Privacy](https://github.com/basecamp/policies/blob/d39f6f17bbd90d82b1df9fa7a0df845edd487de0/privacy/index.md),
  dated 2023-04-20.
- [Reuse permission](https://github.com/basecamp/policies/blob/d39f6f17bbd90d82b1df9fa7a0df845edd487de0/README.md#steal-these-policies).
- [CC BY 4.0](basecamp-policies-CC-BY-4.0.md), copied unchanged from the source license, including its warranty
  disclaimer. The live policy footer links to the original source and license.

The adaptation removes Basecamp-specific names, billing rules, provider practices, locations, deletion promises,
service commitments, and legal status. It preserves substantial account responsibility, acceptable-use, content,
intellectual-property, termination, warranty, and liability terms, with express limits for non-excludable rights.
Privacy sections retain the source's useful structure while leaving actual practices for the operator to supply.
Unknown operator, contact, effective date, providers, retention, and other operating details remain bracketed.
No governing jurisdiction, minimum age, arbitration process, or privacy practice is selected by the starter.

Complete the drafts against the actual application before inviting real users. Review the terms for the actual
operator and audience, including the broad liability exclusion. Signup acceptance does not complete the policies
or establish consent to unrelated data processing. Edit the ordinary views and locale text; no policy engine or
new configuration object is required.

relicense unrelated application code, branding, user content, or other material. The repository's other license
notices continue to apply to their respective material.
